FIELD NOTE01

One model. Explicit assumptions.

Angle is measured away from the surface normal (0° is vertical). The nozzle outlet is modeled as a circular rim rotating about its centroid. The rim’s vertical drop is r · sin(θ), equivalently √(r² − (r · cos(θ))²); the layer height must meet or exceed that value. This is a conservative exit-face clearance check, not a full nozzle-body or machine collision model.

Bead volume approximation

Extrusion uses the capsule-like cross-section A = (w − h)h + π(h/2)², where w is line width and h is layer height. Widths smaller than height are rejected. Physical markers print the test ID, H (height), W (width), M (multiplier), and A (angle); G-code comments repeat the settings.
